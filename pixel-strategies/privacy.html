Pixel Strategies privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-30

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Pixel Strategies on Android, who collects it, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we (Ratio Games) do not run any servers for the game, do not ask you to create an account with us, and do not sell your data. The game uses a few Google services, mainly for ads, purchases and optional cloud saves, and Unity's online services for optional online multiplayer. Those services collect some information as described below.

1. Who we are

Pixel Strategies (Google Play package com.ratiogames.pixelstrategies) is made by Ratio Games, a small independent game studio. You can reach us at ratiogamestudio@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your saved games, settings (such as volume and language), tutorial progress and the record of whether you bought "Remove Ads" are stored locally on your phone, in the game's private app storage. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it (unless you use cloud saves, see section 6).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is paid for by ads. It shows full-screen ads between matches and offers optional rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC, or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).

To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable ID provided by Android);
  • your IP address, which Google may use to estimate your approximate location (for example your country or city). The game does not ask for or use precise GPS location;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • device information and diagnostics, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and performance and crash data related to ads.

Google uses this information to show ads, to measure how ads perform, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to detect and prevent fraud and abuse. Depending on your consent choices (see below) it may also be used to personalise ads. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you buy "Remove Ads", the game stops showing ads between matches and lets you claim bonuses without watching an ad. The ads SDK may still be started when the game launches (for example to keep your consent settings up to date), but no ads are shown to you through it.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation for all apps, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Consent and personalised ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google (the Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. There you can choose whether Google and its advertising partners may use your data for personalised ads (ads based on your interests and activity) or only for non-personalised ads (ads based on general context such as the app and your approximate location). Non-personalised ads still use some data, for example for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. Where required by law in other regions, a similar choice may be shown.

You can change your choice at any time in the game: open Settings → Purchases & privacy → Privacy options. This button appears in regions where Google requires it.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase, "Remove Ads". Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game whether you own the purchase (so it can be restored if you reinstall), and Google shares order information with us as the developer through the Google Play Console, as for any Play purchase (for example the order id, the product, the price, the date and your country). Payments are covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy. The purchase is processed through the Unity In-App Purchasing library, which talks to Google Play on your device; we have not connected it to any Unity online service.

6. Sign-in and cloud saves (Google Play Games)

The game can use Google Play Games to back up your saved games. When you are signed in to Play Games, the game uses your Play Games profile (such as your player ID and gamer name) to identify you and stores copies of your save files (the autosave and your manual save slots, which contain only game progress) in your own Google account, using Play Games Saved Games (kept in a hidden app-data area of your Google Drive storage). We do not have access to your Google account or to these files; they are stored and protected by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in, the game works normally and your saves stay on the device only.

7. Online multiplayer and game engine (Unity)

Online multiplayer is optional and uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay) from Unity Technologies. Nothing is sent to these services unless you choose Find match, Create private game or Join with code on the Multiplayer screen. When you do:

  • the game signs you in anonymously: Unity creates a random player ID for this installation. You don't enter an email, password or any account details;
  • the lobby holds your player ID, the colour you picked and a display name. In public matches the name is a random one such as "Player 1234"; in private matches (joined with a code you share) it is the name you typed;
  • during the match, the moves and game state are passed between the players' devices through Unity's Relay servers, which see your device's IP address to connect it. Other players don't see your IP address.

Unity processes this data to run the online match, under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. The game has no chat. Hot-seat multiplayer (several players on one phone) stays on your device.

Game engine: like other Unity games, the engine itself may send limited technical information about the device (such as hardware model and operating system) to Unity Technologies when the game first runs. We have not enabled Unity Analytics or Unity Cloud Diagnostics.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account with Ratio Games.
  • We do not run our own servers for the game, and we do not add our own analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except as described above (the Google and Unity services that the game relies on).
  • The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

9. Children

Pixel Strategies is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Pixel Strategies → Storage → Clear storage), removes all local saves and settings.
  • Cloud saves: kept in your Google account until you delete them. You can delete them in the Google Play Games app (open your profile, then Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and choose Pixel Strategies under the individual games' data), or in Google Drive on the web under Settings → Manage apps → Pixel Strategies → Delete hidden app data. Menu names may change slightly as Google updates its apps.
  • Advertising and Google data: retained by Google under its own policies. You can manage it at myaccount.google.com and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Online multiplayer (Unity): lobbies are temporary and are removed by Unity once they are empty or inactive. Your anonymous player ID is kept by Unity until it is deleted; clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it makes the game use a new one. To have your Unity player data deleted, email us (we can delete it from Unity's dashboard).
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and in our Play Console records as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal obligations.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you. You can ask us to delete them.

To ask about or request deletion of any data related to the game, email ratiogamestudio@gmail.com. Because we do not hold game data on our own servers, most data can be deleted directly by you as described above; we will help with anything else.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or under Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. For data processed by Google, please use Google's tools and contacts linked above; for anything relating to us, email us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds a feature or a service changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game's update notes on Google Play.
