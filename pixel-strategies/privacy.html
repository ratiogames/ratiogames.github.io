Pixel Strategies privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-09

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Pixel Strategies on Android, who collects it, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we (Ratio Games) do not ask you to create an account with us, do not have servers of our own, and do not sell your data. The game uses a few Google services, mainly for ads, purchases, cloud saves, sign-in and notifications, and Unity's online services for online multiplayer, ranked matches, friends, chat, clans and the season pass. Those services collect some information as described below.

1. Who we are

Pixel Strategies (Google Play package com.ratiogames.pixelstrategies) is made by Ratio Games, a small independent game studio. You can reach us at ratiogamestudio@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your saved games, settings (such as volume and language), tutorial progress, the record of whether you bought "Remove Ads", the name and colour you use in private online games, and your list of recently met online players (the last 20) are stored locally on your phone, in the game's private app storage. We do not receive this information. Chat messages in lobbies and matches are kept only in the game's memory during a match and are forgotten when you leave it or close the game (clan chat is stored, see section 12). Uninstalling the game deletes all of this (unless you use cloud saves, see section 6).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is paid for by ads. It shows full-screen ads between matches and offers optional rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC, or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland).

To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable ID provided by Android);
  • your IP address, which Google may use to estimate your approximate location (for example your country or city). The game does not ask for or use precise GPS location;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • device information and diagnostics, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and performance and crash data related to ads.

Google uses this information to show ads, to measure how ads perform, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to detect and prevent fraud and abuse. Depending on your consent choices (see below) it may also be used to personalise ads. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you buy "Remove Ads", the game stops showing ads between matches and lets you claim bonuses without watching an ad. The ads SDK may still be started when the game launches (for example to keep your consent settings up to date), but no ads are shown to you through it.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation for all apps, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Consent and personalised ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google (the Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. There you can choose whether Google and its advertising partners may use your data for personalised ads (ads based on your interests and activity) or only for non-personalised ads (ads based on general context such as the app and your approximate location). Non-personalised ads still use some data, for example for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. Where required by law in other regions, a similar choice may be shown.

You can change your choice at any time in the game: open Settings → Purchases & privacy → Privacy options. This button appears in regions where Google requires it.

5. Purchases, season pass and skins

The game offers optional in-app purchases: "Remove Ads", a season pass (one per season, which unlocks the premium rewards of that season and turns off ads between matches until the season ends) and skins (cosmetic looks for units and buildings, sold as packs or single pieces). All of them are one-time purchases; there is no premium currency and there are no random paid boxes. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game what you own (so purchases can be restored), and Google shares order information with us as the developer through the Google Play Console, as for any Play purchase (for example the order id, the product, the price, the date and your country). Payments are covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy. Purchases go through the Unity In-App Purchasing library, which talks to Google Play on your device.

Season pass and skin purchases are checked on the server. After you buy one, the game sends the product id and Google Play's purchase token to our Cloud Code script (section 7). The script asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid, then adds what you bought to your Unity player account. To stop one purchase from being used on several accounts, it stores a record of each redeemed token (a one-way hash of the token, never the token itself, with your player ID, the product and the time). "Remove Ads" is not sent to our server; Google Play alone tells the game you own it.

Pass progress and cosmetics are stored in your protected Unity Cloud Save data, which only our script can change: the season, your pass XP and level, a short log of recent matches you reported for XP (time, XP and match id, used for the daily limits), weekly challenge progress, the rewards you claimed, starting-bonus tokens, which seasons' premium pass you own, the skin pieces, badges and titles you own, what you have equipped, and your preferred multiplayer map. Other players see your equipped skins, badge and title in online matches, lobbies, the Friends list and the leaderboard. The season pass and skins work with an anonymous Unity account too (section 7); in that case they are tied to that account, so sign in to Play Games if you want to keep them on another device.

6. Sign-in and cloud saves (Google Play Games)

The game can use Google Play Games to back up your saved games and to identify you online. Signing in is optional: without it you can play the whole single-player game, hot-seat games and private online games. When you are signed in to Play Games:

  • the game uses your Play Games profile (your Play Games player ID and gamer name) to identify you;
  • it stores copies of your save files (the autosave and your manual save slots, which contain only game progress) in your own Google account, using Play Games Saved Games (kept in a hidden app-data area of your Google Drive storage). We do not have access to your Google account or to these files; they are stored and protected by Google under the Google Privacy Policy;
  • your Play Games profile is linked to your Unity player account (section 7), so you can play ranked matches, add friends, type in chat and join clans (sections 8 to 12). Your gamer name is shown to other players there. We never receive your Google account password or email address.

If you are not signed in, your saves stay on the device only, and online you can only play private games.

7. Online multiplayer and game engine (Unity)

Online features use Unity Gaming Services from Unity Technologies: Authentication (player accounts), Lobby and Relay (matches), Friends, Leaderboards, Cloud Save (storage) and Cloud Code (small scripts we wrote that run on Unity's servers, see section 13).

  • Player account: when the game opens its main menu, it signs you in to Unity Authentication so that friend requests and invites can reach you. If you are not signed in to Play Games, this is an anonymous account: Unity creates a random player ID for this installation, and you don't enter an email, password or any other account details. If you are signed in to Play Games, the Unity player account is linked to your Play Games profile and its player name is set to your gamer name (Unity adds a number such as "#12345", which makes your friend code).
  • Lobbies: a lobby holds your player ID, the colour you picked, your team, whether you queued together with a friend, and a display name. In public matches the name is your Play Games gamer name (public matches need Play Games sign-in). In private matches (joined with a code or a friend's invite) it is the name you typed, or a random one such as "Player1234" if you didn't type one; if you are signed in to Play Games, the other players in the lobby can also see your gamer name so they can add you as a friend.
  • During a match, the moves, the game state and chat messages are passed between the players' devices through Unity's Relay servers, which see your device's IP address to connect it. Other players don't see your IP address.

Unity processes this data to provide these services, under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Hot-seat multiplayer (several players on one phone) stays on your device.

Game engine: like other Unity games, the engine itself may send limited technical information about the device (such as hardware model and operating system) to Unity Technologies when the game first runs. We have not enabled Unity Analytics or Unity Cloud Diagnostics.

8. Ranked matches and the leaderboard

Every public online match is ranked. Ranked play needs Play Games sign-in. For it, our Cloud Code script stores in Unity Cloud Save:

  • your rating: your Elo score, the number of ranked matches you have played, the season, and a count of results you reported that didn't match the other players' (to spot cheating). You can read it, but only our script can change it;
  • a record of each ranked match: the player IDs and teams of the human players (taken from the lobby), each player's report of the final standings (places, and who left the match), when it was reported, and the rating changes. Other players can't read these records.

The leaderboard (Unity Leaderboards) shows your gamer name, rating and tier to everyone who plays the game (the top 50, and the players around you). It is reset at the start of each season.

9. Friends and invites

Friends need Play Games sign-in and use the Unity Friends service. It stores your friend list, friend requests you sent and received, and the players you blocked. Anyone who knows your friend code (your gamer name plus a number) can send you a friend request. Your friends can see your presence: whether you are online, in a lobby or in a match (you appear offline about a minute after the game goes to the background).

Friends can invite you to a private lobby, or to a party (two friends who queue together for public team matches). An invite contains the inviter's name and what is needed to join (the lobby code and map, or the party). Invites are delivered inside the game and, when the game is closed, as a phone notification (section 10). They expire after 10 minutes. A party exists only while both of you have the game open; it is not stored.

Blocking a player removes them from your friends, stops their requests and hides their chat messages from you.

10. Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

To tell you about invites while the game is closed, the game uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service. When you are signed in to Play Games, Firebase gives your device a notification token (an ID that lets a notification be delivered to this app on this device). Our Cloud Code script stores the token, with the time it was last updated, in your protected Unity Cloud Save data, which other players cannot read. It is used only to send you a notification when a friend invites you to a lobby or a party, or for your clan (section 12): when a player asks to join your clan (sent to the Leader and Admins), when a clan invites you, or when a clan accepts your request. We don't send advertising or other notifications, and clan chat messages don't trigger notifications. A notification shows the name of the player who sent it (and, for clans, the clan's tag and name). We have not enabled Firebase Analytics or any other Firebase feature.

On Android 13 and newer, the game asks once for permission to show notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings → Apps → Pixel Strategies → Notifications; invites and clan news then only show while the game is open. Google processes the token under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

11. Chat, reports and moderation

Who can chat: typed chat is available in private lobbies and private matches (to everyone, or to your team in team modes) and in public team matches (to your team only). Public free-for-all matches have no chat. Only players signed in to Play Games can send messages; others can read them. Messages are at most 120 characters long, and no more than about one per second.

Word filter: the game checks every message on your device when it is sent and again when it is received. Offensive words in English and Turkish are masked with ***, and links and long numbers are not allowed.

Not stored: messages are relayed to the players in the chat channel through the device of the player who hosts the match and Unity's Relay servers. They are not stored on any server; each player's game keeps the recent messages in memory during the match only. The exceptions are reports (below) and clan chat, which is stored (section 12).

Reports: you can report a player or a message (choose a reason and, if you like, add a short note). A report sends to our Cloud Code script, which stores in Unity Cloud Save: your player ID, the reported player's ID and name, the reason, your note, where it happened (lobby or match), the time, and up to the last 20 chat messages you could see (each with the sender's ID and name, the channel, the text and the time). We (the developer) review reports ourselves. A report is deleted once we have reviewed it. To limit abuse, your player data also keeps the times and targets of the reports you sent in the last hour.

Sanctions: if a player breaks the rules, we may mute them (1, 3 or 7 days, or permanently) or ban them. A mute stops them from sending chat messages; a ban disables their Unity player account, so they can't use any online feature. The sanction is stored in the player's protected Unity Cloud Save data: the mute end date, whether they are permanently muted or banned, and a history of up to 30 moderation decisions (date, action and length). Other players' games check whether players in their lobby are muted, so that muted players' messages are not passed on; they see nothing else from this record. If you think a sanction is a mistake, email us.

12. Clans

Players signed in to Play Games can create and join clans (groups of up to 30 players). Creating a clan needs the current season's premium pass. Clans are run by our Cloud Code script, which stores the following in Unity Cloud Save:

  • The clan: its name, tag (2 to 4 letters or digits, shown as [TAG]) and emblem (a pattern and two colours), its join settings (open, by request or by invitation only, and a minimum rating), who leads it, who created it and when, and when it was last renamed. The name, tag and emblem are public: any player can find the clan by searching, see it in the clan rankings, and see the tag next to its members' names in lobbies, matches, friends lists and the leaderboard. Names and tags are checked against the same English and Turkish word filter as chat.
  • Members: each member's player ID, gamer name, role (Leader, Admin or Member), the date they joined and the date of their last role change, and their ranked rating. Any player who opens the clan's profile can see its members' names, roles and ratings. Your own player data also keeps which clan you are in, when you joined, the end of any waiting time before you can join a clan again (24 hours after leaving or being removed), the seasons in which you created a clan, the clans you asked to join and the invitations you received.
  • Join requests and invitations: a request holds your player ID, gamer name, rating and the time; an invitation holds the invited player's ID and name, who invited them and the time. The clan's Leader and Admins see them. They expire after 7 days.
  • Clan points and rankings: when a ranked match you played as a clan member is final, the script adds points to your clan by your place in that match. For the current season it keeps, per clan, the points, the ids of the last 200 credited matches, and for each member the points they brought, the number of ranked matches credited and their rating at the last one. The clan rankings (by clan rating and by points) are public. At the end of each season the final standings are kept as a record of that season, and members who contributed get a reward (badge, title and pass XP) that is stored with their pass data (section 5).
  • Clan chat: unlike other chat, clan chat is stored on the server so that members can read it later. The script keeps the last 100 messages of each clan (sender's player ID and gamer name, text and time), plus short automatic lines such as "joined" or "was promoted". Older messages are deleted as new ones arrive, and the whole chat is deleted when the clan is disbanded. Only current members can read it. Messages are filtered (offensive words are masked before they are stored) and follow the same rules as other chat (120 characters, about one per second, muted players can't send). To enforce the rate limit, the script keeps the time of each member's last message. Messages you wrote stay in the clan chat after you leave until they are pushed out by newer ones.
  • Reports: you can report a clan (for example for an offensive name). The report stores your player ID, the clan's id, [TAG] and name, its emblem and its Leader's ID, the reason, your note and the time, in the same report queue as other reports (section 11), and is deleted once we have reviewed it. Clan chat messages can be reported like any other chat message. To limit abuse, your player data keeps the times of the clan reports you sent in the last hour.
  • Moderation: if a clan's name, tag or emblem breaks the rules, we may rename it (to a neutral name such as "Clan 1234", after which the Leader can choose a new name once without waiting) or disband it. Mutes and bans (section 11) also apply to clan chat.

When a clan is disbanded (by its Leader, by moderation, or when its last member leaves), its details, member list, requests, invitations and chat are deleted, and its name and tag become free for others. Season records and rewards already earned are kept.

13. Who sees your information, and what we do not do

  • Other players see what the game shows them: your display name or gamer name, colour, team and moves in your matches; your chat messages in your channels; your rating and tier on the leaderboard; your equipped skins, badge and title; your clan's tag (and, on its profile, your name, role and rating); and, if you are friends, your presence.
  • Service providers process data for us as described above: Google (AdMob, Play Billing, Play Games, Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services and the game engine). AdMob also uses data for its own advertising purposes, as explained in sections 3 and 4.
  • We (Ratio Games) can see the data stored in our Unity project (player accounts, ratings, match records, pass progress and owned items, clans and clan chat, reports and sanctions) through Unity's dashboard and our own moderation tool, and we use it only to run the online features and purchases, keep ranked play fair and handle reports.
  • We do not ask you to create an account with Ratio Games, and we never receive your Google password or email address.
  • We do not have servers of our own. Our only server-side code is a few small Cloud Code scripts that run on Unity Gaming Services (for ratings, notifications, reports and sanctions, the season pass and purchases, and clans). We do not add our own analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except as described in this policy, or where the law requires it.
  • The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

14. Children

Pixel Strategies is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

15. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Pixel Strategies → Storage → Clear storage), removes all local saves, settings and the recent players list.
  • Cloud saves: kept in your Google account until you delete them. You can delete them in the Google Play Games app (open your profile, then Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and choose Pixel Strategies under the individual games' data), or in Google Drive on the web under Settings → Manage apps → Pixel Strategies → Delete hidden app data. Menu names may change slightly as Google updates its apps.
  • Advertising and Google data: retained by Google under its own policies. You can manage it at myaccount.google.com and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Lobbies, invites, parties and chat: lobbies are temporary and are removed by Unity once they are empty or inactive; invites expire after 10 minutes; parties and chat messages in lobbies and matches are not stored.
  • Clans: a clan's data is kept while the clan exists and deleted when it is disbanded (section 12). Clan chat keeps only the last 100 messages. Join requests and clan invitations expire after 7 days. Each season's final clan standings and the rewards earned are kept as records of that season.
  • Unity player account and the data linked to it (player ID, player name, rating, leaderboard entries, friends, requests and blocks, notification token, pass progress, owned skins, badges and titles, redeemed purchase records, clan membership, report log and any sanction record): kept until the account is deleted. An anonymous account is no longer used once you clear the game's storage or uninstall it (the game then creates a new one); an account linked to Play Games is used again whenever you sign in. The notification token is also deleted when Firebase reports it as no longer valid (for example after you uninstall the game). Past seasons of the leaderboard may be kept as archived versions.
  • Ranked match records: kept while they are needed for the ratings and to look into cheating or disputes.
  • Reports: kept until we review them, then deleted. The moderation decision is kept in the reported player's sanction record (above).
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and in our Play Console records as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal obligations.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you. You can ask us to delete them.

To ask about or request deletion of any data related to the game, email ratiogamestudio@gmail.com (tell us your friend code, shown on the Friends screen, or your gamer name). We will delete your Unity player account and everything linked to it (rating, leaderboard entries, friends, notification token, pass progress, owned items and sanctions), remove you from your clan (if you lead it, leadership passes to another member as when a Leader leaves; if you are its last member, the clan is disbanded), delete your messages from the clan chat, and remove your player ID from ranked match records, clan records and reports. If you were banned, we may keep the minimum needed to keep the ban in force. Most other data can be deleted directly by you as described above; we will help with anything else.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or under Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. For data processed by Google, please use Google's tools and contacts linked above; for anything relating to us, email us.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds a feature or a service changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game's update notes on Google Play.
